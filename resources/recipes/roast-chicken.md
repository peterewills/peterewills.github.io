# Simple Roast Chicken

## Chicken
* 1 whole chicken (4-5 lbs)

## Dry Rub
* 1 Tbsp kosher salt
* 1 tsp whole black peppercorns
* 1 tsp garlic powder
* 1 tsp dried rosemary
* 1 tsp dried thyme

## Gravy
* Pan drippings
* Potato starch (about 1 Tbsp to start)
* 2 Tbsp cold water or chicken stock per Tbsp of starch
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Pinch of rosemary and thyme (optional)


## Instructions

1. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Set it uncovered in the fridge for about an hour to dry out the skin.

2. Heat the oven to 400°F.

3. Put the salt, peppercorns, garlic powder, rosemary, and thyme in a spice grinder and grind to a coarse powder.

4. Rub the dry rub all over the skin of the chicken.

5. Cover the ends of the legs and the wing tips with tin foil so they don't burn.

6. Roast at 400°F until the thickest part of the thigh reads 165°F, about 1 hour 15 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes for a 4-5 lb bird.

7. Let the chicken rest for 10-15 minutes before carving. Make the gravy while it rests.

### Gravy

1. Pour the drippings into a saucepan. Skim off excess fat if you want, or leave some for flavor. Bring to a simmer.

2. Mix the potato starch with cold water or chicken stock to make a slurry (about 1 Tbsp starch to 2 Tbsp liquid). Mixing it first prevents clumping.

3. Whisk the slurry into the simmering drippings a little at a time. It thickens fast, so stop when you hit the consistency you want.

4. Season with salt, pepper, and a pinch of rosemary and thyme.


**Notes:**
- Potato starch thickens more strongly than flour, so start with less than you think you need. It also stays clearer than flour-based gravy.
- Pairs nicely with mashed potatoes. Mash boiled potatoes with olive oil, almond milk, nutritional yeast, salt, and pepper to keep them dairy-free and gluten-free.
