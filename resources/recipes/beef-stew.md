# Beef Stew

## Protein & Base

* 3.5 lbs beef stewing meat (sirloin or round), trimmed and cut into inch cubes
* ¼ tsp freshly ground pepper
* 2 tsp vegetable oil

## Liquids & Aromatics

* 1 bottle (750 ml) dry red wine
* 3 cups beef broth, homemade or low-sodium canned (plus more if needed)
* 4 Tbsp tomato paste
* 2 bay leaves
* 1 tsp dried thyme
* ½ tsp dried rosemary, crushed
* 4-6 cloves garlic, minced
* 2 tsp salt

## Vegetables

* 3 lbs frozen pearl onions
* 5 medium carrots, peeled and cut into ¼-inch rounds
* 3 celery stalks, chopped
* 16 oz mushrooms, quartered
* 24 oz baby potatoes, halved

## Finishing

* Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 325°F.

2. Brown the beef: Season beef with pepper. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Brown beef in batches (don't overcrowd), about 5 minutes per batch. Remove beef and set aside.

3. Reduce the wine: Stir tomato paste into the pot and cook until it darkens to brick red, about 2 minutes. Add the wine, scraping up any browned bits with a wooden spoon. Boil until reduced by about half, 10-12 minutes.

4. Build the base: Return beef to pot. Add beef broth, bay leaves, thyme, and rosemary. Stir to combine.

5. Add vegetables: Add half the pearl onions, carrots, celery, mushrooms, potatoes, and garlic. Season with salt. Stir everything together and bring to a simmer.

6. Cover and bake: Put the lid on and transfer to the oven. Cook for 3 hours at 325°F.

7. Check liquid and add pearl onions: After 2 hours, stir in the remaining pearl onions (no need to thaw). If the stew looks dry, add ½-1 cup more broth. Return to the oven for the final hour.

8. Finish: Remove from oven. The beef should be fork-tender and the stew thick and rich. If too thin, simmer uncovered on the stovetop for 10-15 minutes. If too thick, add a splash of broth or water.

9. Final touches: Remove bay leaves. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Stir in fresh chopped parsley. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.

**Serves:** 4
